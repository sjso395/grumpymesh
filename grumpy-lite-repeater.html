The Grumpy Lite Repeater

The Grumpy Lite Repeater is our streamlined, more affordable solar-powered MeshCore repeater. It uses the same Grumpy Mesh enclosure, but with a stripped-down internal design and fewer features than the Grumpy King Repeaters — giving you the core repeater capability at a more attractive price.

Inside the Grumpy Lite

We've taken the Grumpy Mesh design and simplified it without losing the features that matter most. The Grumpy Lite is built around a compact, efficient setup designed for outdoor and off-grid MeshCore deployments.

  • Heltec V4 screenless radio — 27 dBm transmit power with built-in Wi-Fi
  • 11,000 mAh battery capacity — two 21700 batteries
  • 10-watt solar panel — designed to provide solar charging for off-grid operation
  • Weatherproof USB connector — for programming and service
  • N-Female bulkhead connector to IPEX — a clean antenna connection from the radio to the external antenna
  • Choice of Wi-Fi antennas — select the configuration that fits your installation
  • Choice of rear enclosure — select the back configuration that works for your installation
