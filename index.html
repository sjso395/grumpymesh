🚀 Introducing the Grumpy Jr Repeater

A More Affordable Way to Get Grumpy

We're developing a new repeater designed to bring the Grumpy Mesh experience to more installations at a more attractive price.

The Grumpy Jr Repeater uses our same Grumpy Mesh enclosure, but with a stripped-down internal design and fewer features than the Grumpy King Repeater. Inside, it will feature a Heltec V4 screenless 27 dBm radio with Wi-Fi, two 21700 batteries providing approximately 11,000 mAh, a 10 watt solar panel, weatherproof USB, and an N-Female bulkhead-to-IPEX antenna connection.

You'll also be able to choose your Wi-Fi antenna and rear enclosure configuration.

More information will be released in the next few weeks!
