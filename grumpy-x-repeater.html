The Grumpy X Repeater

A new repeater we have been designing in the lab, the "X" repeater will be a "cross" between the Grumpy King Repeater and the Grumpy Lite Repeater.

Introducing the Grumpy X

The Grumpy X is being designed to give us the best of both worlds. It will bridge the gap between the Grumpy King and Grumpy Lite while giving us a flexible platform that can evolve as the GrumpyMesh lineup grows.

  • Interchangeable board design — the internal board can be swapped so a Grumpy Board can be added later.
  • Heltec V4 to start — the initial configuration will use a Heltec V4 to help keep the price lower and provide an option when the Grumpy Board is not available.
  • Over 16,000 mAh of battery capacity — built around 21700 batteries for extended off-grid operation.
  • King + Lite crossover — designed to combine key ideas from both the Grumpy King and Grumpy Lite Repeater.
  • Built for flexibility — the design allows the repeater platform to grow with future GrumpyMesh hardware.
STAY TUNED FOR MORE ANNOUNCEMENTS!The Grumpy X is currently being designed and tested in the lab. Specifications, features, and final configurations may change as development continues.
