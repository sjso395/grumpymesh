The Avery Repeater
A Grumpy Mesh project dedicated to the memory of Avery Natal.

The Avery Repeater will be a small form-factor repeater designed for HOA communities or for hanging in trees at hunting leases, for example. It will feature a smaller solar panel and smaller battery while maintaining similar Grumpy Board specifications.

Still under development — We are still in the lab putting this one together, but we will make it special!
